Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 31, 2026

Your privacy matters to us. This policy explains what information Spendly collects, how it is used, and the choices you have.

1. Data We Collect

When you use Spendly, we collect:

  • Account information — your name, email address, and a securely hashed password.
  • Expense data — the amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions you enter.
  • Basic usage information — such as login times, used to keep your account secure.

We do not collect bank account numbers, card details, or access to your financial institutions.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used only to:

  • Provide and operate your Spendly account.
  • Generate the spending summaries and category breakdowns you see in the app.
  • Communicate with you about your account, such as password resets.
  • Maintain the security and reliability of the service.

We do not sell your personal data or use it for advertising.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored on secured servers and is accessible only to you through your account. Passwords are never stored in plain text. We retain your data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, your personal and expense data will be permanently removed.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly does not share your personal or expense data with third parties for marketing purposes. We may rely on trusted service providers, such as hosting providers, to run the service. These providers process data only on our behalf and are required to protect it. We may also disclose information if required to do so by law.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or want to request access to or deletion of your data, contact us at privacy@spendly.example.
